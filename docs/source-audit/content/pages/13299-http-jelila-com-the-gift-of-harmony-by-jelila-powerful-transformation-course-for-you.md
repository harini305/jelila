🔮 The Reprogramming 

The Reprogramming is Jelila’s belief‑change modality, far deeper and more comprehensive than NLP or ThetaHealing. It focuses on one specific area of life at a time, uncovering and permanently shifting the hidden beliefs that drive unwanted patterns. Unlike general techniques, The Reprogramming is focused on a specific topic of your choice, and fully resolves the chosen topic throughout all parts of the self. This is what makes it different: it brings permanent resolution to the issue, ensuring that the transformation is complete and lasting.

The Reprogramming, a unique technique created by Jelila, starts with a talking process to identify your negative beliefs, then goes into an energy healing to transform them. 

The session is focused by your initial answers to three specific simple questions, designed to highlight your most pressing issues. Gentle intuitive healing helps you process some of the integration. After your session, you’ll receive a list of your beliefs, and the new positive beliefs which replace them, to take home.

📘 Landmark Forum 

The Landmark Forum is a three‑day intensive program (plus one evening) offered worldwide. It grew out of Werner Erhard’s EST trainings of the 1970s, which were longer and more expensive, and was redesigned in the 1990s to be more accessible. Costing around USD $650–700, Landmark is known for creating rapid breakthroughs in perspective, relationships, and productivity. Participants often report freedom from limiting beliefs and a new sense of possibility. Unlike The Gift of Harmony, Landmark is group‑based and focuses on awareness of thought structures rather than a staged permanent integration.

—

🗣️ NLP (Neuro‑Linguistic Programming) 

NLP is a collection of techniques that reprogram thought and behavior through language patterns. It is taught in trainings and workshops worldwide, with costs ranging from USD $200 to $2,000+ depending on certification level. NLP is known for improving communication, persuasion, and emotional regulation. It offers a toolbox of methods rather than a unified staged journey, making it flexible but less focused on permanent transformation compared to Gift of Harmony.

—

✨ Access Bars 

Access Bars is an energetic modality involving gentle touch on 32 points of the head. Sessions typically cost around USD $150 and are available in over 100 countries. It is known for clearing mental clutter, reducing stress, and opening space for creativity. While deeply relaxing, Access Bars works session by session and does not provide a structured multi‑part framework like Gift of Harmony.

—

🌌 ThetaHealing 

ThetaHealing uses meditation to access the theta brainwave state and reprogram subconscious beliefs. Sessions cost between USD $75–200 and practitioners are found worldwide. It is known for spiritual healing, trauma release, and abundance work. While powerful for belief change, ThetaHealing is delivered in individual sessions without a staged overarching process, unlike Gift of Harmony’s permanent resolution model.

—

🌬️ Breathwork 

Breathwork encompasses practices such as holotropic, rebirthing, and clarity breathing. Sessions or retreats cost between USD $50–150 and are widely available globally. Breathwork is known for emotional release, stress reduction, and accessing altered states of consciousness. It is highly effective for catharsis and self‑awareness, but remains episodic, lacking the structured, staged framework for permanent integration that defines Gift of Harmony.

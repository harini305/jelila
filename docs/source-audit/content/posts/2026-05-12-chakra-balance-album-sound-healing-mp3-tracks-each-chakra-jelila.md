✨ Understanding the Chakras 
The word chakra comes from Sanskrit, meaning “wheel.” These seven spinning centres of energy run from the base of the spine to the crown of the head, each linked to a colour, element, and aspect of life:

🌈About The Chakras

- ❤️ Root (Muladhara) – Base of spine Grounding, safety, stability

- 🧡 Sacral (Svadhisthana) – Lower abdomen Creativity, sexuality, flow

- 💛 Solar Plexus (Manipura) – Upper abdomen Confidence, willpower, vitality

- 💚 Heart (Anahata) – Centre of chest Love, compassion, connection

- 💙 Throat (Vishuddha) – Throat Expression, truth, communication

- 💜 Third Eye (Ajna) – Between eyebrows Intuition, insight, clarity

- 🤍 Crown (Sahasrara) – Top of head Spirituality, unity, transcendence

When these chakra centres are in harmony, prana or chi (life energy) flows freely, supporting physical health, emotional balance, and spiritual growth.

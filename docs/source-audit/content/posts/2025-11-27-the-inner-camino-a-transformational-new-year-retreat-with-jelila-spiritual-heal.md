# The Inner Camino™ – A Transformational New Year Retreat with Jelila, Spiritual Healer in Bali🌿

[![Soul healing in Bali with Jelila &#8211; Manifest your dreams](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1000093540-1.jpg)](https://jelila.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1000093540-1.jpg)

As the year turns and the world leans into celebration – twinkling lights, overflowing tables, the joyful noise of family and festivity – there is another rhythm quietly calling. A quieter path. The New Year. A Retreat. A deeper question…

What next?

For centuries, pilgrims have walked the Camino de Santiago at this time of year, braving winter winds and bare frosty landscapes to seek something more than the season’s abundance. They walk not for gifts or glitter, but for grace – for the hush between heartbeats, for the whisper of the soul beneath the noise of the world.

And I have walked that whisper in Peru – across the vast altiplano, along long train tracks with only llamas and alpaca for company. I’ve tasted how coca leaves and mesquite can make the stars seem to spin. I’ve flown above the extraordinary etched figures and lines of Nasca, their mystery humming through the desert like a forgotten song. I’ve climbed the hills to feel the wisdom held in the monumental stones of Machu Picchu, Cusco, and Arequipa – sacred places where the earth itself speaks in silence.

And here in Bali – long revered as a global sanctuary for spiritual healing, trauma release, and past life integration – the land hums with a frequency that softens the veils between worlds. Many seekers arrive with questions they can’t quite name, and leave with something they didn’t know they’d lost: the feeling self, the playful self, the doing self – three sacred aspects of being that so often go missing in the rush of modern life.

But even the outer Camino, for all its beauty and challenge, is only the beginning…

Cue the refrain – that iconic, windswept whistle from a cowboy film, heralding the arrival of the lone rider. The hero. The one who dares to go deeper. 🌵🏜️🔫

🌟 Part Two: The Inner Camino

Let the late morning sun over the closing year guide your footfalls to the *Inner Camino* – a soul-map drawn not on parchment, but in stardust and shadow. Along this path, we’ll journey together through sacred terrain:

🌲 The Forest of Tears 
Here we pause to feel, to soften, to cradle the tender places. We find the lost inner child, coax her out with laughter and light, and invite her to dance again in joy.

⛰️ The Mountain of Ego 
A steep climb where thunder cracks and lightning strikes the hardened Rock of the Masculine. In the storm, the domineering maniac dissolves, revealing the kind and benevolent provider within.

🌀 The Southern Whirlpool 
A swirling sea of sorrow and longing. We dive deep to rescue the Sodden Feminine, drenched in loss and despair. With tenderness, we lift her from the depths and place her gently on the Podium of Honour, where she rises – radiant, joyful, and sovereign once more.

🕳️ The Cave of Self-Neglect 
A shadowed hollow where the Higher Self lies forgotten, thin and weary. We enter with lanterns of love, nourish this sacred being, and return him to the circle – where his wisdom can once again guide the way.

☀️ The Sunny Dome 
Our final destination. A golden sanctuary where all four parts of you – Doing, Feeling, Playful, and Wise – gather in harmony. Here, they dream together, weaving the luminous tapestry of your life’s next chapter.

You may encounter these parts of yourself like quiet refugees, emerging from shadow during your journey. They may appear in dreams, in tears, or in sudden moments of knowing – guiding you to recognise their long-held needs. If that happens, know that I am here. I have already forged the path to their restitution, and I call it The Gift of Harmony™.

This is not a journey of miles, but of miracles. And when you’re ready, I’ll be here to walk it with you.

With reverence and joy, 
Jelila 🌿

🔧 Part Three: The Process – How the Inner Camino Transforms You

The Inner Camino is not just a poetic metaphor – it is a living, breathing New Year retreat in Bali. A soul-led process of transformation I’ve developed over 28 years, guiding clients through emotional healing, trauma release, and past life integration into true inner harmony.

🔓 1. The 11 Core Beliefs – Rewriting the Soul’s Script 
At the root of suffering lie 11 deep subconscious beliefs I have identified – ancestral, societal, and karmic imprints that whisper (in very specific sacred wordings not shared here):

- “I’m not enough.”

- “I must please to be loved.”

- “I can’t trust myself.” …and more.

Through precise, guided processes, your chakra system reprocesses and transforms these beliefs – replacing them with truths that liberate your essence. This is not surface-level mindset work. It is soul-level reprogramming.

Because your belief system is hierarchical – like a tree or root🪾🌳 system – when you change something at the trunk level, the entire structure above it collapses. This allows for profound transformation without years of therapy – which often doesn’t reach this depth.

If you’re a Spiritual Explorer🪶– someone who enjoys the journey but isn’t seeking real change – this may not be for you.

But if you are a Spiritual Warrior🗡️ – one who bravely confronts inner dragons in the name of freedom – then this is your path.

This is a map of your inner world, where each square represents a key relationship dynamic between the four core aspects of self: Doing, Feeling, Playful, and Wise.

These “beautiful words,” as one client called them, are recorded and anchored in your aura. They become your new truth. A simple 5-minute daily practice – The Gift Tool™ – helps you maintain this inner harmony.

💬 3. The Sacred Transaction – Relearning the Language of Love 
At the heart of every relationship is a conversation – not just with others, but within yourself.

Most of us were never taught how to let our inner Masculine and Feminine speak with clarity, respect, and love. This is why so many relationships falter – we’ve never seen true inner harmony modelled.

Through the Successful Relationship Transaction™ – a unique communication tool I’ve developed – you’ll learn how to create joyful relationship moments, both within and without.

You’ll shift from: 
“Why haven’t you taken the rubbish out again?” 
to: 
“I’d love it if we could sit by the fire and you could put your arms around me.”

You’ll learn how to identify your true needs and express them in a way that inspires action – not resistance. Your Inner Masculine becomes engaged. Your real-world relationships begin to reflect this new inner alignment.

This is how you stop projecting. This is how you start co-creating. This is how your soul’s goals become real.

## Are You Committed, to Your Makeover?

It’s a bit like walking into a salon and saying, “I’d love a two-tone colour, a glamorous updo, a cut, conditioning treatment, and a manicure.” The stylist replies, “That’ll be $300.” And suddenly, the doubts creep in: Will it work? Will I like the change? What if I feel worse afterwards? So you say, “Actually, I’ll just take the $25 manicure.”

You’ve avoided the risk of disappointment – but you’ve also missed the chance to look and feel utterly fabulous. You didn’t consider the risk of not looking amazing. So now, you’re heading to the party with just a manicure, when you could’ve had a full-blown makeover. Isn’t that a kind of loss too?

When making decisions – especially about healing – you need to weigh the risk of not doing it alongside the risk of doing it.

## 💫 In Healing, Consider This:

The cost of doing it (or doing a cheap one):

- You’ll save money – but you might lose out on the abundance that deep healing could unlock.

- You’ll stay comfortable – but you’ll continue to feel discomfort when your unresolved issues get triggered.

- You’ll avoid disappointment – yet you may feel disappointed when relationships or goals don’t work out due to unhealed patterns.

- You won’t face the challenge – but you’ll keep battling negative mind chatter, stress, and distraction.

- You’ll avoid emotional intensity – but you might overreact in key moments, damaging relationships or your work environment.

Do you see what I mean? We often evaluate only the cost, the potential difficulty, or the fear of change. That can lead us to choose sessions that are shallow or safe – but not transformative.

And in doing so, we forget to consider the benefits we’re missing by not going deeper. That’s where the real transformation lies.

Lemurian Dreaming is my song about Lemuria, a civilisation around the time of Atlantis, about 13,500 years ago. Lemuria was the last time when people were balanced within – the doing, feeling, playful and spiritual parts of the self were in harmony. Rather than being dominated by the masculine, doing part of the self as most people are now.

## 💔 What Is Trauma – and How Can You Truly Release It?

Trauma isn’t just about the story or event – it’s about the feelings you didn’t allow yourself to feel at the time. Jelila explains:

> “Repressed emotions – guilt, loneliness, sadness – are stored in a kind of basket. When something similar happens, or when you simply begin to feel again, the basket opens, all the feelings spill out – and it feels like the traumatic experience is happening all over again.”

This is why traditional talk therapy often falls short. Jelila’s Past Life Journey gently guides you to float above the event, revisit it safely, and name and release the blocked emotions—without needing to relive the pain.

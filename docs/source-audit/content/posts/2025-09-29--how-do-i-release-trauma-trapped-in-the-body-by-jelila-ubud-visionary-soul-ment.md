🧱 What Is Trauma?

Trauma is the energetic repression of feelings. Instead of allowing emotions to move through you, they’re boxed up—stored in your energy field. This “trauma box” contains unacknowledged emotions linked to a specific experience.

And here’s the key: 
That box can be triggered to open by similar experiences. 
When a present-day situation echoes the emotional tone of the original event, the box spills open. Suddenly, you’re flooded with the same feelings, as if you’re reliving the past.

The universe, sensing this stored energy, may even send more of the same—mistaking it for a request.

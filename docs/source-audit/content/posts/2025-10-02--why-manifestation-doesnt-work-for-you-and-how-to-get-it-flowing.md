# ✨ Why Manifestation Doesn’t Work for You—And How to Get It Flowing

[![2026 Manifestation Mastery Course with Jelila &#8211; getting control get what you want in your life in Ubud, Bali](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/manifestation-mastery-with-jelila.jpeg)](https://jelila.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/manifestation-mastery-with-jelila.jpeg)

You’ve probably heard that manifestation works like magic. Think it, feel it, attract it. And yes—it absolutely can. But if you’ve been trying to manifest your desires and feel like something’s off… you’re not alone.

Here’s the missing piece most people don’t realise:

You’re not just one voice inside. You’re nine.

🌈 Meet Your Inner Council

In my unique framework, The Gift of Harmony, I guide you to meet the nine parts of your self:

– Four core aspects: Doing, Feeling, Playful, and Wise

– Four more that emerge in specific life areas

– And behind them all… Soul, your deepest essence

Most people try to manifest from just one part—the thinking mind. But that’s only 1/9 of your inner orchestra. Imagine trying to convince nine friends to go to the same movie. Chances are, you’d split into two groups and see different films!

That’s what happens inside you when your parts aren’t aligned. Some may be excited about your dream… while others quietly resist, hold fear, or carry outdated beliefs.

🧠 Outdated Beliefs That Quietly Say “No”

These inner “no’s” don’t shout. They whisper.

They sound like:

– “I’m not worthy of that.”

– “It’s too late for me.”

– “I have to struggle to succeed.”

– “People like me don’t get what they want.”

These beliefs don’t always come from your conscious mind. They may live in your Feeling self, your Playful self, your Wise self… and yes, even your Soul.

Soul, while deeply wise, can carry karmic imprints, ancestral echoes, or vows from other lifetimes that quietly block your desires. It’s not about blame—it’s about awareness. When Soul says “no,” it’s often trying to protect you based on an outdated story.

That’s why your manifestation might feel blocked. It’s not that you’re doing it wrong—it’s that some parts of you are still holding onto old scripts.

🌟 So How Do You Get All Nine On Board?

In a healing and manifestation session, I gently guide you to connect with each part of your self. We ask:

What do you truly feel about this desire?

Is there any resistance, fear, or conflict—even from Soul?

Then, using The Reprogramming—a powerful technique I created—we release hidden blocks and rewire your subconscious beliefs. It’s like tuning your inner symphony so every part plays in harmony with your dream.

When all nine parts say yes, manifestation flows naturally. You become magnetic to your desires—not just mentally, but soul-deep.

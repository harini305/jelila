# The World is Turning – Message from Jelila – 21/12/2010

[![](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/journeys-header-only-small.jpg)](https://jelila.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/journeys-header-only-small.jpg)

**The World is Turning** by Jelila 21/12/2010

[![Jelila Collection - Crystal Healing Ubud Bali - www.jelila.com - Sanctuary Wallpaper](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/jelila-collection-crystal-healing-ubud-bali-www-jelila-com-sanctuary-wallpaper.jpg)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/JelilaHealing)The world is turning, turning, and those who have been reviled, unjustly piled upon by the trashes of civilizations, endless mocking of hearts feeling and constant transmission of dread from news reports of misery and deprivation from every direction …

…are suddenly free!

The wind is blowing, flying in the golden hairs of angels, hearts open, and suddenly we see…

That there is nothing to worry about!

[![Jelila Collection &#8211; Crystal Healing and Readings Ubud Bali &#8211; www.jelila.com &#8211; spiritcloth.typepad.com &#8211; blue heart](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/jelila-collection-crystal-healing-and-readings-ubud-bali-www-jelila-com-spiritcloth-typepad-com-blue-heart1.jpg)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/JelilaHealing)Spiritcloth – weaving together

[![www.jelila.com www.jelila.wordpress.com](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/ds-angel-3.jpg)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/JelilaHealing)…and patient angels in the form of you and I, are piecing back together our civilization, our collective shattered heart, our very love, hour by hour, minute by minute, in stitched frames on the internet, tiny images, moving colour and words, all singing our illuminated story, on God’s great new gift, the new social systems, the new technology, the mechanism for the re-making and re-weaving, of what we lost in the Fall – our greatness, our wisdom, our very humanity – the wisdom from our experience.

[![Jelila Collection &#8211; Crystal Healing and Readings Ubud Bali &#8211; www.jelila.com &#8211; earth_cloth](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/jelila-collection-crystal-healing-and-readings-ubud-bali-www-jelila-com-earth_cloth.jpg)](https://www.etsy.com/shop/JelilaHealing)Earth Weaving

Giving us back the ability to share, to love, to know, that all this information, this knowledge, this feeling, this very life – is ours, belonging to all, created by the many, for the one that we really are!

So weave you well, drip all the golden honey of your beloved creations into the pot – share them, love them, stir and mix it up, and when time comes, we’ll take a deep draught or a well-rounded ladleful of our shared sweetness, sipping deeply from my creation and yours, natural fulfillment of each other’s answers, of each other’s desires, bathing in our own rich goodness~!

The Hidden Pain Beneath Emotional & Physical Struggles

Behind anxiety, depression, and even chronic physical conditions lies emotional repression, often stemming from childhood experiences. A child who faces mistreatment or neglect from their parents struggles to comprehend such rejection. Instead of processing their pain, the child subconsciously redirects it elsewhere – manifesting lifelong patterns of self-sabotage, chronic illness, and emotional distress.

This psychological mechanism often keeps people stuck, unknowingly blocking their healing. Jelila says, “Many clients tell me they had a ‘wonderful childhood,’ yet moments earlier, they recalled vivid incidents of criticism, neglect, or abuse.” This contradiction reveals a protective instinct – an unwillingness to accept past mistreatment because it conflicts with their deep-seated belief that they are lovable.

Therapies Often Fall Short

Many therapies focus on discussing the past but rarely access the deeply buried emotions that create ongoing suffering. If healing attempts haven’t worked, it’s often not the fault of the healers – but the subconscious resistance to uncovering painful truths.

Some clients unknowingly protect their trauma from themselves, preventing them from truly healing. Superficial approaches – endless talking therapy, generic energy healing, or temporary mood shifts -don’t reach the deeper psychological and emotional reprogramming required for lasting change.

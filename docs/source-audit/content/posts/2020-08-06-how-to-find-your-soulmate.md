## Why are Soulmate Relationships so Hard?

At first, a soulmate reflects back to you the beautiful parts of yourself you’ve forgotten or denied. You feel lit up, amazed, in love – but really, you’re falling in love with a lost piece of your own soul that’s ready to return.

Then comes the harder part: they begin to mirror the aspects of yourself you don’t like, the ones you’ve buried. That’s when things get tricky. You might feel triggered, or they might. Sometimes one of you runs. Yet this stage is actually a gift – because it shows you the pieces of yourself that are waiting to be healed and reclaimed.

# 🧠 Reprogramming the Mind: A Systems Approach to Inner Harmony – Permanently Transform Mental Patterns, Blocks and Conflicts – Jelila

What if you could apply logic and computing principles to the mind? 
To truly take control of yourself – and your life?

This is exactly what I wondered when I arrived in Ubud, Bali – the global epicentre of personal growth and transformation. There, I was gifted a method for reaching into the subconscious mind and changing hidden beliefs. What I discovered was astonishing: the mind operates as a belief system, and it behaves strikingly like a computer.

🧩 The Mind as a System

In Bali, I quickly realised that our subconscious is structured like a software programme. It contains “statements of truth” – core beliefs – that silently drive our behaviour, decisions, and even the events we attract. These beliefs manifest outcomes in our lives, often ones we don’t consciously choose or understand.

When I was given access to this inner system, I did what any systems designer would do: 
I began to identify its components.

What makes up the human operating system? 
What are the modules, the subroutines, the feedback loops?

🧠 The Breakthrough: Multiple Inner Selves

One day, during a session with a client, something extraordinary happened. He began speaking with an alternate voice – one that didn’t know what another part of him had just said. It was as if two separate programmes were running in parallel, unaware of each other.

That moment was a breakthrough. I realised: 
There are distinct “selves” within each person – each with its own belief system.

These inner selves often conflict, creating internal “bugs” that manifest as anxiety, indecision, or self-sabotage. I began mapping these parts, understanding their roles, and identifying the dysfunctional beliefs that kept them in conflict.

🌈 The Gift of Harmony: Ending the War Within

This understanding became The Gift of Harmony – a powerful process that aligns the four main parts of the self. It’s like running a deep diagnostic and then harmonising the system architecture. The result?

- Negative mind chatter stops

- Overthinking dissolves

- Inner voices become supportive, not critical

Clients report astonishing shifts in just three days. Why? Because the process works at the root level – deep within the subconscious code.

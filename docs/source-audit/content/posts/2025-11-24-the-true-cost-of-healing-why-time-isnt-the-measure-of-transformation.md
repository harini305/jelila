# The True Cost of Healing: Why Time Isn’t the Measure of Transformation – Jelila

[![Your chakra system &#8211; colours and light that processes &#8216;like a washing machine&#8217; &#8211; Jelila](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1000091754.jpg)](https://jelila.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1000091754.jpg)

🌿 In a world where doctors are often expensively paid – costly not only in financial terms to you and to society, but also in the emotional and physical toll of what you must endure – it’s time to reframe what healing truly means. Physical disease is not random. It’s the soul’s final cry – a manifestation of unmet trauma, repressed emotions and limiting beliefs that have gone unaddressed for too long.

The real cause of illness is not the body’s failure, but our reluctance to look inward. Many people are afraid to feel – yet how you feel is your true compass. Learning to feel is what can save you from burnout. It gently guides you toward the right direction for you, in your life. If your thinking, doing self drags you toward more and more achievement – but it doesn’t match your heart’s true longings – that misalignment can also make you sick.

And what most people don’t yet realise is that there are now elegant, graceful processes for releasing these inner blockages. Healing doesn’t have to be painful, embarrassing or uncomfortable. It can be beautiful – a gentle return to emotional truth – a relief.

Even people who have wept buckets and faced truly challenging issues in healing sessions with me often say afterwards, “That was fun!” That’s partly due to my natural humour and tone – but it’s also because focusing on yourself, with someone guiding you gently through it, is deeply rewarding. It’s exciting, inspiring and motivating to discover why certain unwanted patterns have shown up in your life, how they got there and how to undo them. That insight brings new knowledge and a fresh ability to create more of what you truly want. And that’s not just healing – it’s personal empowerment.

As a transformational healer, I guide clients through profound emotional releases, sometimes in just one or two sessions, bringing clarity, sovereignty and deep transformation. Yet ironically, rapid healing is often undervalued. Many still equate time spent with value received, imagining that longer processes must be more effective.

But what if healing could be swift and elegant?

Imagine this: 6 sessions over 6 hours for $800, gently resolving 6 issues – or 1 potent, soul-deep session that dissolves 8 core blocks in just 1 hour, also for $800. Most would choose the longer path, not because it’s more effective, but because we’ve been conditioned to equate time with value.

The same is true of the choice to repress your emotions and deal with them by becoming sick. It takes a long time – and costs you far more.

It’s a curious paradox. Many undervalue their own time, forgetting that faster healing means more hours reclaimed for joy, creativity or income. The true gift isn’t in the duration – it’s in the liberation.

If you’re ready to reclaim your time and transform with grace, I invite you to explore what’s possible when healing meets sovereignty. Your soul already knows the way – I simply help you listen, and learn how to feel in real time – not ‘afterwards’. This lets you respond better in the moment, and create your better life.

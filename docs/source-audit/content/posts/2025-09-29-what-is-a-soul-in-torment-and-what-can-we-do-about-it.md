In my popular online healing practice, I often meet clients who feel deeply unsettled – out of sync with life, unsure whether their time here is even worth engaging with. Not because they lack ambition or opportunity, but because something deeper is misaligned. Their soul is in torment.

---

The Soul’s Silent Suffering

We rarely speak about how our soul feels. Yet beneath the surface of our daily lives, we are infinite beings – souls here to express, create, and evolve. When that expression is blocked, misunderstood, or fragmented, the soul suffers. It may feel trapped, unheard, or unable to connect with the rest of the self. This can manifest as melancholy, demotivation, or a sense of futility.

One client described it perfectly: “I don’t know whether it’s worth doing things in the time available.” The time available – being one’s life, of course.

---

What Causes Soul Torment?

Soul torment often arises from: 
– Misconceptions within the soul’s belief system 
– Internal conflicts between parts of the self 
– Energetic blocks that prevent authentic expression

When the soul cannot fully inhabit the body or align with its purpose, it may feel stuck, sluggish, or even suffer. This is not just emotional – it’s existential.

This kind of soul torment is especially common among those who carry responsibility for others – therapists, coaches, and increasingly, HR leaders. As emotional stewards of the workplace, HR professionals often suppress their own soul discomfort in service of the collective. But their healing matters too.

# 🌟 Are You Secretly Afraid to Finish Things? How to stop Procrastinating – Jelila

[![2026: The Gift of Harmony with Jelila &#8211; &#8216;profound life transformation in just 3 days&#8217; in Ubud Bali. &#8216;Unique experience&#8217;](https://jelila.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1000083865.jpg)](https://jelila.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1000083865.jpg)

Do you find it hard to complete projects, wrap things up, or even say a clear “no”? 
Do you start with passion, only to get tangled in distractions or delays?

You’re not alone. The fear of finishing — of completing, ending, or succeeding — is surprisingly common. And often, it’s not laziness or lack of discipline. It’s something deeper.

It’s a fear of success.

But why would we fear success?

From guiding tens of thousands of clients, I’ve discovered that in the subconscious, “success” often means incarnating into the world. 
Soul got in. “Yay, success!” I arrived.

And yet — many people carry a hidden resistance to that moment. A part of them whispers: 
“Success, or being here, isn’t safe. It’s not a good thing.”

Strange, isn’t it?

Why would birth — our first act of creation — feel like failure?

🌍 Because there’s a collective memory. 
A soul-echo of lifetimes spent in slavery, servitude, or suppression. 
A belief that says: “I always have to do what I don’t want here. I always suffer. I don’t want to do that anymore.”

So what we’re really facing is a resistance to being alive. 
A feeling that being here is a failure — because we can’t do what we truly want.

This is one of the core human programs I’ve identified. 
It’s like dragging a boulder uphill — this subconscious weight that pulls you backwards. 
And it’s one of the deep patterns we release in my 3-day immersion for Spiritual Masters, The Gift of Harmony, or in Deep Reprogramming sessions.

🌀 Daniel’s Journey — The Fear of Finishing

In one Reprogramming and Past Life Journey, a radiant client named Daniel arrived tangled in a web of beliefs. 
He couldn’t finish anything. His life was littered with half-begun projects. 
He was constantly battling “distractions” — but they were really soul-level diversions.

His birth story held a clue.

Daniel was due on April 1st — April Fool’s Day. 
His mother tried to delay the birth to April 2nd, fearing the baby would be seen as foolish or ridiculous.

His soul purpose? “Looking for solutions.” 
But when I asked, “What problem are you trying to solve?” 
He paused. And then the belief emerged:

✨ “I’m an unexpected problem.”

He was trying to solve himself.

This led him to manifest endless problems — because we project what we believe we are. 
Our “I am…” beliefs shape our world. 
As I gently said to him, “You’re generating them.” 
His eyes widened behind his glasses. And he smiled.

---

🌸 Beliefs Born at Birth

Here’s what was playing out:

- “My mother doesn’t want my creation to be a joke — so she’s delaying my birth.”

- “My birth is ridiculous.” (Meaning: unsafe to implement.)

- “I’m afraid of looking ridiculous around others.”

And “mother” here doesn’t just mean your biological mum. 
It’s your inner feminine — the part of you that holds dreams, feelings, love, and lifetimes.

Daniel’s subconscious was saying: 
“It’s not safe to finish anything. Later would be safer.” 
But “later” never comes.

🧬 My Own Birth Story

My mother tried not to give birth on December 31st (my actual birthday). 
She wanted to be in the papers for giving birth on January 1st.

So the belief might be:

- “I’m avoiding my birth to be famous.” Which creates: avoiding creation, and not being famous.

I also heard:

- “My fascinating notion isn’t safe and free.” (Where “fascinating notion” = what I’m here to create.)

So: “What I’m here to create is enslaving me.” 
And: “I’m not safe to be in the papers.” 
Which can manifest as fear around documents, visibility, or recognition.

Another layer:

- “I’ve scarpered before I came to the place of birth.” Meaning: “I’m running away before I’ve even been born!”

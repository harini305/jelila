What Your Scores Reveal

Write your numbers down. Notice which areas feel nourished – and which are calling for more attention. You may find you’re over-invested in one domain while others quietly yearn for expression.

Did your scores feel balanced? Or did one world dominate?

In Western culture, we often over-focus on the Physical World (Doing) – but within you, there are three other vital aspects:

– The Doing One. 
– The Feeling One 
– The Playful One 
– The Wise One

These parts need equal expression for your life to feel meaningful and satisfying.

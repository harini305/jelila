## 🌍 The Base Chakra – Foundation of Your Being

The **Base Chakra**, also known as the root chakra, is the energy centre that grounds you into reality. When it is balanced, you feel safe, secure, and able to build a strong foundation in life. When it is out of balance, you may feel ungrounded, unstable, or disconnected from the world around you.

Its colour is a deep, dark red – the shade of earth and vitality. The symbol of the base chakra has **four petals**, representing stability, and it is linked to the **cube**, the most stable of the sacred geometric shapes. This connection reminds us that the base chakra is about structure, support, and the ability to stand firmly in life.
